# Changelog

All notable changes to Roulette Royale are documented in this file, following the [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/) format.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-24

### Added

- A 3D European roulette table in a casino: a lacquered mahogany wheel with a brass turret, chrome frets and diamonds, and an ivory ball; a green baize layout under a low table lamp; a lit tote board of recent numbers; and a dim casino floor of slot machines, chandeliers, gaming tables and marquee lights.
- A simulated ball that rides the rim, spirals down the bowl, glances off the diamonds, and bounces between the frets before it settles in a pocket.
- Every European bet: straight up, split, street, trio, corner, first four, six line, column, dozen, red, black, odd, even, 1–18 and 19–36, placed by clicking or tapping the felt, with table limits.
- Chips of 1, 5, 25, 100 and 500 credits, with undo, clear, rebet and double, and right-click or press-and-hold to take a stack back.
- Winning bets paid beside their stacks, losing chips swept, and a dolly on the winning number.
- A camera that follows the game from the layout to the wheel to the winning pocket, plus seated, wheel and overhead views.
- Quick spin, which runs the wheel at double speed without changing the outcome.
- Credits, history and settings saved in your browser, with a refill when you run out. Play credits only: no real money and no purchases.
- Keyboard controls, and touch controls sized for phones in both orientations.
- An attract mode that plays the table by itself behind the menu.
- Synthesised casino sound effects with a mute toggle.
- Deployed to GitHub Pages on every push to `main`.
